# INDUSTRIAL FASTENER STANDARDS AND SPECIFICATIONS

## 1. Material Grades and Strengths
- Grade 2 Bolts: Made from low or medium carbon steel. Tensile strength is 74,000 PSI. No markings on the bolt head.
- Grade 5 Bolts: Made from medium carbon steel, quenched and tempered. Tensile strength is 120,000 PSI. Head marking consists of 3 radial lines.
- Grade 8 Bolts: Made from medium carbon alloy steel, quenched and tempered. Tensile strength is 150,000 PSI. Head marking consists of 6 radial lines. Highly recommended for heavy duty industrial or structural applications.
- Stainless Steel 18-8 (304): Excellent corrosion resistance. Tensile strength is 70,000 to 100,000 PSI.

## 2. Thread Sizing and Pitches
- Unified National Coarse (UNC): Standard coarse threads. E.g., 1/4"-20, 3/8"-16, 1/2"-13. Recommended for general locking in soft materials.
- Unified National Fine (UNF): Standard fine threads. E.g., 1/4"-28, 3/8"-24, 1/2"-20. Used in high-vibration applications.
- Metric Threads (ISO): Spaced by pitch in millimeters. E.g., M6x1.0, M8x1.25, M10x1.5.

## 3. Plating and Finishes
- Zinc Plating: Standard rust protection (clear or yellow chromate). Provides 72 to 96 hours of salt spray resistance.
- Hot-Dip Galvanized: Thick zinc coating for outdoor use. Offers superior corrosion resistance but requires oversized nuts to fit.
- Black Oxide: Decorative finish with light oil lubrication. Little to no corrosion resistance.
